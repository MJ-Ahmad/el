# English Learning Roadmap

Welcome to the **English Learning Roadmap Project**!  
This project is designed for Bengali speakers who want to learn English step by step — from **Beginner** to **Expert** level.  

---

## 🎯 Project Goals
- Build confidence in speaking English.
- Learn through small, progressive steps.
- Practice daily with interactive activities.
- Track progress with timeline and resources.

---

## 🟢 Beginner (Class 1–5)
- Greetings & Politeness  
- Daily Needs & Small Conversations  
- Time, Place & Feelings  
- Teacher–Student Roleplay  
- Storytelling & Presentation  

👉 Outcome: Speak short sentences and basic conversations.  

---

## 🟡 Intermediate (Class 6–10)
- Weather & Daily Routines  
- Small Talk & Group Discussion  
- Mini Presentation  
- Roleplay Activities  
- Conversation Expansion  

👉 Outcome: Talk about daily life and routines in English.  

---

## 🔵 Advanced (Class 11–15)
- Debate Practice  
- Opinion Sharing  
- Detailed Storytelling  
- Public Speaking  
- Interview Practice  

👉 Outcome: Express opinions, debate, and present confidently.  

---

## 🟣 Expert (Class 16–20)
- Professional Emails  
- Formal Presentations  
- Cultural Expressions  
- Fluency Drills  
- Advanced Discussions  

👉 Outcome: Use English fluently in professional and cultural contexts.  

---

## 📂 Resources
- Flashcards for vocabulary  
- Interactive games  
- Progress tracker (timeline.json)  
- Practice activities  

---

## 🏆 Final Outcome
By following this roadmap, you will:  
- Start with basic greetings.  
- Progress to daily conversations.  
- Advance to debates and presentations.  
- Achieve fluency and confidence in English.  

---
